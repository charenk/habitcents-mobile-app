The six categories

InteractionIntuitive gestures, fluid transitions, purposeful haptics, zero-friction core flows3 / 5

Strengths

  • The core loop is genuinely one tap. A daily skip or slip is a single unconfirmed tap (CheckInCard.tsx:119-125); "Change answer", a partial-slip sheet, and missed-yesterday backfill keep speed from costing correctness.
  • Expense logging is two taps minimum; the form auto-expands only when an amount exists, with merchant autocomplete (AddExpenseSection.tsx:93-99).
  • Haptics are purposeful and restrained: exactly 3 call sites, all money moments (save, keypad, check-in), centralized in utils/motion.ts:40-47.
  • The expenses drag sheet snaps by gesture velocity like a real sheet (TodayExpensesPanel.tsx:115-127).
  • Onboarding is skippable, resumes mid-flow, and the guided log writes a real first expense.

Gaps, ranked by impact-to-effort

  • ADA-001 P0 crash on the Habits tab for a fresh install in release builds; the core loop is unreachable for a new TestFlight user. Fix already staged in worktree fix/habits-tab-crash (KeptHero.tsx:66-75 mixed animation drivers, plus worklet serialization in 3 files).
  • ADA-002 Settings is fully built but unreachable: hidden from tabs and never pushed. Currency, Restore Purchases, Privacy, and Terms have no entry point (app/(tabs)/_layout.tsx:73-78).
  • ADA-011 Failures land in silent .catch(() => {}); no user-facing error state exists anywhere.
  • ADA-012 Loading is the word "loading"; contexts hydrate after first paint and the splash is never held to bridge it (app/index.tsx:21-27).
  • ADA-025 Stock transitions only; no continuity or shared-element polish.
  • ADA-026 Haptic vocabulary stops at success and light; destructive and selection moments are silent.
Why 3: the one-tap loop is award-grade on its own, but a P0 crash on its home tab, an unreachable settings surface, and silent failure handling are disqualifying at award level.
Visuals and graphicsTypography hierarchy, color system, iconography, spacing rhythm, motion design3 / 5

Strengths

  • Color is a real, disciplined token system. constants/theme.ts is the single source, consumed via useTheme() in 52 of 57 UI files, with semantic families documented in place: neutral slip states, tier badge bg+ink pairs, the SpendPulse heat ramp, habit-class badges. Principles are encoded: green positive-only, shape + label never color alone.
  • Motion has a philosophy: concentrated on the two money moments, every non-celebration timing inside 100-250ms (full inventory in appendix A).
  • One icon family used consistently: Ionicons, 47 rendered instances, no mixing.
  • Complete icon suite: light, dark, and tinted iOS variants, Android adaptive + monochrome, configured splash.

Gaps, ranked by impact-to-effort

  • ADA-006 No typography scale: 323 hardcoded fontSize literals across 20 distinct sizes; 13, 14, and 15 used near-interchangeably (53, 63, 50 times). Weights are top-heavy ('600' x90, '700' x81, '400' x1), flattening hierarchy.
  • ADA-007 No spacing or radius tokens: 18 distinct radii; 12 is the de-facto card radius (52 uses) with 16 and 14 fighting it on sibling cards.
  • ADA-014 No shared primitives: zero Button, Card, Text, or Sheet components; about 42 files hand-roll touchables, which is the mechanical cause of the drift above. Two different components are both named HabitCard.
  • ADA-013 Two animation systems coexist: Reanimated 4 in newer components, classic Animated in older ones; the mixed-driver pattern is also the ADA-001 crash source.
  • ADA-016 Dead surface and stale docs: expo-linear-gradient and the fabGradient tokens are unused; CLAUDE.md and DESIGN.md still claim dark mode and chart components that no longer exist.
Why 3: the color system shows award-level intentionality, but typography and spacing run on unmanaged literals, and the system-font, stock-component look reads functional rather than distinctive.
Delight and funMoments of surprise, micro-interactions, personality without gimmicks3 / 5

Strengths

  • The kept-dollars celebration is honest and earned. KeptHero counts up with a scale + green tint pulse only on increase, and announces the settled value to VoiceOver. It celebrates money kept, never spend.
  • The skip micro-interaction stacks three cheap signals into one satisfying beat: press spring, expanding 550ms ring, success haptic (CheckInCard.tsx:91-106), then the week-strip dot pops.
  • Save morphs to a check for 550ms before resetting (AddExpenseSection.tsx:147-155).
  • Personality lives in the writing: the reveal number carries a mandatory tilde and an honesty line; empty states coach rather than apologize.

Gaps, ranked by impact-to-effort

  • ADA-027 Delight stops at the two money moments: milestones (1, 3, 7, 14, 30, 66 days), first leak plugged, and first kept week all pass unmarked.
  • ADA-012 Waiting moments are dead air, the cheapest place personality is missing.
  • ADA-026 No tactile personality beyond the three haptic sites.
Why 3: what exists is tasteful, honest, and gimmick-free, but the surface area is thin: two good moments, then nothing for the rest of the app.
InclusivityVoiceOver, Dynamic Type, contrast, Reduce Motion, localization readiness2 / 5

Strengths

  • A deliberate, tested VoiceOver layer. utils/a11y.ts builds labels from the accessibility matrix spec and is unit-tested for exact wording; decorative icons hidden, icon-only buttons labeled, selection states marked.
  • Reduce Motion is exemplary. A live hook (utils/motion.ts:19-37) gates every animated component; haptics intentionally still fire, the correct call.
  • Currency is properly international: integer cents, Intl.NumberFormat per-currency locale, zero-decimal JPY (utils/currency.ts).
  • 44pt calendar targets addressed in the P2-5 pass.

Gaps, ranked by impact-to-effort

  • ADA-003 Every primary CTA fails contrast: white on #4CAF50 computes to 2.78:1 (AA needs 4.5:1 normal, 3:1 large). This pair is the core Skip button, onboarding CTAs, the paywall purchase button, and Save (theme.ts:6).
  • ADA-005 Dynamic Type will clip, not reflow: zero maxFontSizeMultiplier anywhere plus fixed-height containers (tab bar height 84).
  • ADA-004 textTertiary #9E9E9E = 2.68:1 on white; also the inactive tab icon tint. 184 combined secondary/tertiary references, and textSecondary itself sits at 4.34:1 on the app background, just under AA.
  • ADA-023 tierLikelyInk 3.86:1 and calendarDow 3.06:1, both under AA at their sizes.
  • ADA-008 13 hardcoded 'en-US' date formats in 8 files while currency respects locale; an English "Week of" prefix in ReportsContext.tsx:233-244.
  • ADA-009 Strings outside strings.ts, including accessibilityLabel="Amount in dollars", wrong for 6 of 7 supported currencies (AmountInput.tsx:70,87).
  • ADA-010 The WidgetCard trend arrow speaks color only: unlabeled and not hidden (WidgetCard.tsx:269-273).
  • ADA-024 No rendered-tree a11y or Dynamic Type test coverage; the on-device VoiceOver walk is deferred to Phase 4 ratified and must pass before store submission.
Why 2: the VoiceOver and Reduce Motion work is genuinely strong, but the two heaviest criteria fail measurably today: contrast fails on the primary action of every screen, and large-text users get clipped layouts. ADA Inclusivity winners are flawless on exactly these two.
InnovationNovel use of platform capabilities in service of the core job2 / 5

Strengths

  • The Leak Scan is a genuinely novel mechanic: a fully on-device, zero-network CSV pipeline with automatic column and sign inference capped at two user questions, confidence tiers, and a graceful-failure path into the manual flow (app/leak-scan.tsx, utils/leakScan/). Privacy as a design material. This is the app's strongest award story.
  • The no-network rule is real and enforced (single env-gated analytics exception).
  • PrivacyOverlay masks amounts in the iOS app-switcher snapshot, a thoughtful platform-aware touch.

Gaps, ranked by impact-to-effort

  • ADA-017 Reminder toggles capture intent but nothing ever fires: expo-notifications is not installed. For a habit product, timely local notifications are the platform's single highest-leverage capability, and they are absent.
  • ADA-018 Zero home-screen presence: no widget (the kept counter is a born widget), no Live Activity, no App Intents or Siri, no Shortcuts donation. The entitlements file is an empty dict.
Why 2: one original mechanic keeps this off the floor, but ADA Innovation is judged on platform leverage, and the app currently uses iOS as a generic runtime.
State of the platformLatest HIG compliance, native components, dark mode, all device sizes2 / 5

Strengths

  • Current stack: Expo SDK 54, RN 0.81, New Architecture, expo-router 6; safe areas via useSafeAreaInsets() on every top-level screen; keyboard avoidance on all six input sheets.
  • Modern icon suite including the iOS 18 tinted variant; Handoff activity registered; URL scheme wired.
  • Corruption-tolerant storage with backup-on-corrupt reads (utils/storage.ts:33-41).

Gaps, ranked by impact-to-effort

  • ADA-022 Tab bar metrics hardcoded (height: 84, paddingBottom: 28) instead of inset-derived (app/(tabs)/_layout.tsx:16-18).
  • ADA-021 Module-scope Dimensions.get('window') in 2 files, stale after any resize; useWindowDimensions exists for this.
  • ADA-015 Hand-built sheets where the platform has native detents; the drag panel is a PanResponder + spring.
  • ADA-028 Ionicons instead of SF Symbols; expo-symbols would align iconography with the platform, with text-relative weight and scale.
  • ADA-019 No dark mode ratified: a complete darkTheme sits unreferenced with a documented revert path; an ADA submission would need this revisited.
  • ADA-020 iPhone-portrait only ratified: no tablet, no landscape, no size classes.
Why 2: foundations are current, but the app reinvents native surfaces, skips platform iconography, and ships without dark mode or device adaptability, the first two things a judge toggles.
