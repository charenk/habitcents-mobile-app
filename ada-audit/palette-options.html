Why A is the recommendation

A fixes every failing pair while changing the fewest perceived pixels: buttons and tints deepen one step within the hue family the palette already uses (#2E7D32 is the existing tierSolidInk), and the bright brand green keeps its job as the color of celebration (kept pulse, progress arcs) where contrast rules do not bind it. B is the boldest look if you want the palette to feel like a statement. C is the best pure system but trades away warmth. All three land AA on every pair the audit flagged, including the two new failures found while computing (hero numerals, active tab tint).
