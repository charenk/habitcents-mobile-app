Primary green re-tune: three directions

ADR 0027: the primary must carry white. Thresholds: white text 4.5:1 (button labels, the mock's "Save expense"), white icons 3:1 (the quick-log plus, calendar checks). Current sage #4CAF82 is 2.71:1 with white, which is why every label on it went ink in 2026-08-12. Each card below renders the real components in that option's actual values. All ratios are computed WCAG, not eyeballed.

Shared baseline (unchanged in every option)

Ink #1A1D23, slate, mist, cloud, snow neutrals; the kept tint sageLight #E8F5EE; text on the tint keeps using sageDark #2C7851 (4.79:1 on the tint, passes). Only the token that fills buttons, the plus, dots, and active tints changes.

Option A

Promote sageDark

Primary becomes the existing #2C7851. No new color in the system; the palette collapses to fewer greens. Deep forest reading.

Why B is the recommendation

B is the smallest honest move: one token changes value, the hue Charen chose stays exactly, and both thresholds pass. A is the safe overachiever if the darker reading feels right in the mockups above. C preserves the brightest brand at the cost of a permanent two-green rule that still leaves the bright green unusable on small UI. Whichever wins, sageDark keeps its current job as text on the tint, and the pressed state derives by the same darkening step shown in each card.
