# Impeccable project context: HabitCents mobile

## Design Context

### Users

Adults who already know they overspend and are tired of being told so. They open HabitCents
on a phone, usually in a spare moment, often right after a purchase they half-regret. The job
they are hiring the app for: **see the truth about one spending pattern, decide to break it,
and watch the money that decision keeps add up.**

Core loop: log a spend in under 10 seconds, detect the leak, break one habit, count the
dollars kept. Everything is local-only (AsyncStorage, no backend, no Plaid, no network calls
except the env-gated anonymous PostHog exception).

Primary context of use: one-handed, thumb zone, bright and dim rooms, iOS first (TestFlight
beta stage). Light mode only by direction lock (2026-07-02); a complete `darkTheme` is retained
unreferenced in `constants/theme.ts` as the documented v1.x revert path.

### Brand Personality

**Calm, honest, non-judgmental.**

The app is a neutral mirror, not a coach that cheers or scolds. When someone opens it after a
bad spending week, it should feel like quiet confidence: here is what happened, stated plainly,
without shame and without a pep talk. Restraint outranks encouragement.

Consequences that follow from this:

- A slip is never red. A slip is neutral (`theme.slip` = mist). Coral is destructive actions only.
- Spend is never a win. Spend figures are ink, spend bars are mist on snow. Sage never touches
  a spend figure or a border around one.
- Never invent statistics. State observed evidence ("$119.05 across 4 buys"), never a fabricated
  rate. No invented totals ever (ADR 0022): the only accumulated total the app renders is the
  user's own.
- Locked vocabulary: **leak, skip, kept, slip**. Never streak, success, or completed language.
- Sentence case. No em dashes, anywhere, ever. All strings live in `constants/strings.ts`.

### Aesthetic Direction

Editorial restraint on a near-white page. Neutrals carry roughly 90 percent of every screen
(ink, slate, mist, cloud, snow). If a screen feels colorful, something is off.

- **Type**: Instrument Serif is display-only and appears in exactly three places: screen titles
  ending in a period, money (hero / stat / reveal amounts), and Today-view quotes (italic).
  Never body, never buttons. Everything else is Inter on the `typeScale` steps, no sizes off
  the scale. Eyebrows are the only all-caps (11pt, semibold, letterSpacing .88). Every number
  that can sit above another number is `fontVariant: ['tabular-nums']`.
- **Color meanings** are one-to-one and already ratified: sage = a kept outcome or the action
  that produces one; lavender = habit arc, chapters, premium; amber = upcoming money; coral =
  destructive only.
- **Surfaces**: feature card radius 20, list card radius 14, sheets bottom-anchored radius 20
  top with a grab handle, toasts as an ink pill. Radii come from the set {10, 14, 20, 28, 999}.
  One 20pt horizontal gutter per screen, 12pt vertical rhythm inside a view.
- **Decoration budget**: exactly two decorative gradients are allowed in the whole app (the
  premium upsell card hero and the welcome-screen aurora). Scroll-edge fades are functional,
  not decorative, and allowed.
- **Motion budget**: tap 120ms, sheet/toast 220ms, screen 360ms, easing
  `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`. Exactly one playful motion exists (the 280ms skip-confirm
  pulse); a second one needs an ADR. Every animation has a reduced-motion path.

Anti-references: anything that reads as a gamified savings app (confetti, mascots, streak
flames, badge walls), and anything that reads as a bank dashboard (dense KPI grids, sparkline
decoration, red/green profit-and-loss coding).

### Design Principles

1. **The mirror does not editorialize.** Show observed evidence; let the user draw the
   conclusion. No fabricated numbers, no motivational framing, no shame coding.
2. **One meaning per color, one pattern per job.** There is ONE switching pattern, four button
   styles, one add-another affordance. Grow an existing pattern rather than inventing a third.
3. **Neutral by default, accent by exception.** Sage works because it is rare. Overuse of any
   accent kills its signal.
4. **Every tappable thing declares what it does.** Every row carries exactly one trailing
   affordance (chevron = opens in-app, external-link = leaves the app, value = current status,
   none = in-place async action). A row that gives no hint is the bug, not a style choice.
5. **Deviations are allowed; unexplained deviations are not.** Name any departure from the
   pattern vocabulary in the PR body so the reviewer judges the exception, not the surprise.

### Accessibility Bar

WCAG AA is the floor, Apple Design Award craft is the aspiration. Screen titles carry
`accessibilityRole="header"`; switchers are tablist/tab with selected state; targets are 44pt
or padded to it with hitSlop; Dynamic Type caps are 1.5 on chrome and eyebrows, 1.3 on serif
amounts; anything mounted off-screen is hidden from assistive tech; status conveyed by color is
also carried in the accessible label.

Known live constraint: two animation patterns crash only in release builds (JS-thread
`withSequence` assignment, and mixed RN Animated drivers on one node). One driver per node,
no JS-thread sequences.
