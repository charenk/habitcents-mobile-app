1b Redesign · HabitCents design system

The app, redesigned from the ground up.

Same product truths (skip is the win, slips never subtract, everything on-device), rebuilt on the design system: editorial serif numbers, sage as punctuation, calm neutrals, emoji category tiles, Lucide icons. Reference: the current app, end to end (separate page).

1 · Three surfaces, one job each
Today (check in + log), Money (ledger + upcoming), Insights (leaks + reports). Four tabs become three; settings moves behind the gear.
2 · The kept number is the face
Serif, tabular, always first. The check-in sits directly under it, so the daily ritual is one glance and one tap.
3 · One green, used sparingly
Sage marks the CTA, the kept number, and active states only. Slips stay neutral, lavender carries the habit arc, amber flags upcoming bills.
4 · Product grammar from the DS
Emoji category tiles, tabular currency, 10px buttons, 20px cards, hairline borders, soft ink shadows. No gradients except the premium card.
5 · Copy leads with the number
Sentence case, plain verbs, no judgment. "Saved." "Logged." "Tomorrow's a fresh start."
Onboarding, redesignedSame 90-second audit, editorial confidence. The reveal number gets the serif treatment it deserves.
