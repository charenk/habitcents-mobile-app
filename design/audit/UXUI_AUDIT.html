Three decisions before the gated fixes

1 · The palette's ratified AA failures

Mist text (2.80:1) and white-on-sage CTAs (2.71:1) are ratified tokens that fail the bar the system itself declares. Options: darken in place; split roles (mistText vs mistDeco, ink-on-sage labels); or accept and document.

Recommended: split mist into mistText ~#6B7A8F and mistDeco; CTA fill to sageDark #2E7D55 (5.03:1)

2 · Ratify the real scales instead of policing the current ones

fontSize 14 (x24), 16 (x15), 17, 26, 30 have no token; micro radii (3-5, x20) have no token; spacing has no tokens at all and the observed rhythm is 2pt-based. Add the missing steps then enforce hard, or rewrite 50+ call sites to fit the incomplete scale.

Recommended: add label 14, button 16, lead 17, sheetTitle 26, displayMid 30, radii.micro 4, and a spacing export

3 · Performance depth before beta

Minimum: useMemo the 6 unmemoized provider values (Toast first: 10 files re-render twice per toast, and a toast fires on every mutation) plus the CategoriesContext commit-ref port. Medium adds React.memo on rows, Spent virtualization, and a HabitsContext slice split.

Recommended: minimum now (Lane 1, ~1 hour, zero visual change); medium if the device pass shows dropped frames

Findings

What is genuinely working

  • Motion and crash discipline: every animated component has a reduced-motion branch on a single driver, with a comment trail back to the release-crash postmortems.
  • Honesty guards are real code paths in at least six components: evidence floors, refuse-to-extrapolate, unearned-credit refusal, median prefills, the rise-only arc.
  • The primitive layer genuinely enforces: Chip (label + state + hitSlop), SegmentedControl roles, EmojiTile decorative handling, Icon fallback glyph, ConfirmSheet re-entrancy guard.
  • ExpensesContext concurrency is unusually good for a v1: commit-ref pattern, serialized materializer, tombstones, pre-hydration guard.
  • Strings centralization is ~99 percent real, and the row-affordance vocabulary is implemented, not just documented.
  • AuroraBackground is a model decorative layer; undo-not-confirm deletion restores list position, implemented identically twice.
