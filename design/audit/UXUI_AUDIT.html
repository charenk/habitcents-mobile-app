Decisions before the gated fixes

1 · The palette's ratified AA failures Resolved

Mist text (2.80:1) and white-on-sage CTAs (2.71:1) are ratified tokens that fail the bar the system itself declares. Options were: darken in place; split roles (mistText vs mistDeco, ink-on-sage labels); or accept and document.

Shipped (Phase B): CTA fill stays #4CAF82, label flips white to ink (2.71 to 6.24), sagePressed #3D9A6E added for the pressed fill · mist splits into mist (fills) and mistText #677481 (4.78 on white, 4.53 on snow) · amberInk to #8F5500 · coral to #C93B3B (both directions) · sageDark to #2C7851 · disabled labels white to slate

Correction: this report originally claimed the proposed mistText color #6B7A8F scored 4.54 on white and passed. That was wrong: #6B7A8F is 4.37 on white / 4.14 on snow and fails. #677481 is what actually shipped, and it genuinely passes. See the changelog.

2 · Ratify the real scales instead of policing the current ones Resolved

fontSize 14 (x24), 16 (x15), 17, 26, 30 have no token; micro radii (3-5, x20) have no token; spacing has no tokens at all and the observed rhythm is 2pt-based. Options were: add the missing steps then enforce hard, or rewrite 50+ call sites to fit the incomplete scale.

Shipped (Round 1): label 14, button 16, lead 17, sheetTitle 26, displayMid 30 · plus displayLarge 36, quote 20, titleSm 18, micro 9 in a second batch · radii.micro 4 · a new spacing export (2pt rhythm) and a layout export for shared chrome metrics. fontSize literals fell 116 to 2 (both remaining are by-design exceptions).

3 · Performance depth before beta Resolved (minimum)

Minimum: useMemo the 6 unmemoized provider values (Toast first: 10 files re-render twice per toast, and a toast fires on every mutation) plus the CategoriesContext commit-ref port. Medium adds React.memo on rows, Spent virtualization, and a HabitsContext slice split.

Shipped (Round 1): all of minimum, plus most of medium (React.memo on 5 components, Spent + CategoryTransactionsSheet virtualized). Not shipped: HabitsContext's ephemeral-slice split, and the new React.memo is not yet fully effective everywhere (Today's renderItem still passes fresh per-render callbacks). UX-012's synchronous scan pipeline was never in this decision's scope and remains untouched.

4 · Smaller decisions still open, awaiting Charen Still open

UX-041, sheet grab handle: add real swipe-to-dismiss (ADR, new motion behaviour) or drop the handle; the code now flags this in place. UX-040, Money sheet header (partial): the serif sheetTitle treatment already won in code; PATTERN_VOCABULARY.md still needs to ratify it. The "stop breaking" color contradiction: theme.ts documents coral as destructive, naming "stop breaking" explicitly; the shipped trigger renders slate, preserved as-is, needs a ruling. The welcome splash (app/onboarding/welcome.tsx) is still knowingly off ADR 0022 behind a ratify-or-revert comment; only its dead code was cleared.

Findings

What is genuinely working

  • Motion and crash discipline: every animated component has a reduced-motion branch on a single driver, with a comment trail back to the release-crash postmortems.
  • Honesty guards are real code paths in at least six components: evidence floors, refuse-to-extrapolate, unearned-credit refusal, median prefills, the rise-only arc.
  • The primitive layer genuinely enforces: Chip (label + state + hitSlop), SegmentedControl roles, EmojiTile decorative handling, Icon fallback glyph, ConfirmSheet re-entrancy guard.
  • ExpensesContext concurrency is unusually good for a v1: commit-ref pattern, serialized materializer, tombstones, pre-hydration guard.
  • Strings centralization is ~99 percent real, and the row-affordance vocabulary is implemented, not just documented.
  • AuroraBackground is a model decorative layer; undo-not-confirm deletion restores list position, implemented identically twice.
