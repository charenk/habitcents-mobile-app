# TabBar (app/(tabs)/_layout.tsx + components/ui/TabBarIcon.tsx)

## Direction (current)
Four tabs, white, 1px cloud top border, 56pt of content above the safe-area inset. The selected tab is marked two ways, only one of which is colour: its glyph is filled where the others are outlined, and its label is a heavier sage. Nothing sits behind the icon and nothing animates. Settings is not a tab; it opens as a sheet from the gear on Today.

## States
- Active: filled glyph in `theme.primary` inside a 32pt box with no surface, label `uiBold` in `theme.primary`.
- Inactive: outlined glyph at stroke 1.5 in `theme.tabIconDefault`, same 32pt box, label `uiMedium`.
- Large Dynamic Type: labels shrink to fit their column, floored at the 11pt a default-size user sees. Reach: `xcrun simctl ui <udid> content_size accessibility-extra-extra-extra-large`, then relaunch.

## Decisions
- 2026-09-06: the selected tab is a filled glyph, and the pill is gone. Why: the pill was a bordered box floating in the chrome, the heaviest surface in the bar and heavier than the content above it; filled against outlined is the same signal carried by the glyph itself, which is what iOS does. The ADR 0037 requirement is unchanged and still met: verified by desaturating a screenshot, where the solid glyph reads as selected with no colour at all, more clearly than the old 1.12:1 pill fill did. Rejected: keeping the pill and dropping only its border (the escape hatch this file carried, but the surface itself was the problem, not its edge); a solid sage pill with a white glyph (already rejected 2026-09-05, would compete with Today's green plus button). Supersedes the pill half of ADR 0037.
- 2026-09-06: the 2.25 active stroke goes back to 1.5 in both states. Why: the heavier stroke was the pill's partner signal, and under a fill it only bloats the shape. Fill carries far more weight than a 0.75 stroke bump ever did.
- 2026-09-06: `Wallet` and `TrendingUp` carry authored filled variants in `components/ui/TabGlyphsFilled.tsx`; `Sun` and `LayoutGrid` use lucide's own `fill`. Why: lucide forwards `fill` to every subpath, which is correct for closed geometry (Sun's centre circle, LayoutGrid's four rects) and deforms open paths (the wallet collapses into lumps, the trend line into wedges). The authored pair traces lucide's vertices so the focus swap changes weight, never shape. Rejected: swapping Money and Insights to different, fillable lucide glyphs (changes the metaphors to work around a rendering detail); importing a second icon family for solid weights (kept as the fallback if the authored shapes ever fail a device pass). Precedent: the paywall radio swaps `CircleDot` for `Circle` rather than restyling one glyph.
- 2026-09-06: `layout.tabBarHeight` 64 back to 56. Why: the 8pt was added on 2026-09-05 to clear a 32pt pill, and there is no pill. Toast derives from the same token so it drops 8pt with the bar, which is correct. ADA-005 (min-height plus padding) stays open; this neither fixes nor worsens it.
- 2026-09-05: the selected tab gains a pill, a heavier stroke and a heavier label. Why: active sage and inactive mist measure 1.12:1 against each other and the active tab was 14 percent darker, so hue carried the whole state and it vanished in grayscale. Verified by desaturating a screenshot before and after. ADR 0037. **PARTLY REVERSED 2026-09-06: the pill and the stroke gap are gone, replaced by a filled glyph. The grayscale requirement that produced them is unchanged and still met.**
- 2026-09-05: the pill is Chip's `soft` selected tone, not a new pattern. Why: PATTERN_VOCABULARY says do not invent a third switcher, and Chip's record says `soft` exists "for rails where a solid fill would read as a CTA". A solid sage tab would have competed with Today's green plus button. Rejected: solid sage fill with a white icon. **REVERSED 2026-09-06: no surface behind the icon at all.**
- 2026-09-05: no motion. Why: thumb swaps in switchers are instant by house rule; SegmentedControl rejected a sliding thumb as "a second animated surface competing with the sheet and toast motion the spec already budgets"; and the tab bar is a named suspect in the build 5 launch-crash incident. Rejected: a pill that slides between tabs, and a scale on select (a second playful motion needs its own ADR).
- 2026-09-05: the inactive tab reserves the same 56x32 box. Why: a pill that only existed while focused would reflow the row on every tab change. **AMENDED 2026-09-06: the box is 32x32 and the reasoning now covers glyph metrics rather than a pill, but reserving one identical box in both states is unchanged.**
- 2026-09-05: `layout.tabBarHeight` 56 to 64. Why: 56 minus the 8pt top padding left 48pt for a 32pt pill above a label reaching 16.5pt at the 1.5x cap. Toast derives from the same token, so it rose 8pt with the bar, which is correct. **REVERSED 2026-09-06: back to 56 with the pill gone.**
- 2026-09-05: labels use `adjustsFontSizeToFit` with a `1/1.5` floor, **iOS only**. Why the platform guard: under the New Architecture RN's Android path never forwards `minimumFontScale` and falls back to a 4dp floor, so the shrink would make large-text labels unreadable there. Android keeps the cap and truncates instead. Why: the "Ins" and "Cate" clipping was horizontal, not vertical, so the taller bar did not fix it; four tabs give each 98pt and "Categories" needs about 91pt at the capped size. The floor means a large-text user never lands below the default 11pt. UX-067, `docs/device-pass.md` section 6.
- Ratified and unchanged: sage on the active tab (ADR 0018), four tabs, lucide icons, and the inactive tint clearing 3:1 because an inactive tab icon is a meaning-bearing control, not decoration (UX-003).

## Open
- Closed 2026-09-06: the pill's faint fill and heavy border. The whole surface went; the escape hatch this file described turned out to understate the fix.
- ADA-005 asked for the bar to become min-height plus padding rather than a fixed height. Returning the token to 56 leaves that exactly where it was.
- The authored filled glyphs are verified on the iPhone 16 simulator only. Wallet's clasp is a 3pt punched hole and TrendingUp's arrowhead is a small solid triangle; both want a look on a real device, at arm's length, before the store pass.
- Cross-page swipe (a swipe past the last in-page segment moving to the next tab) was proposed and rejected on 2026-09-06. Why: the boundary between "next segment" and "left the page" is invisible, segment counts differ per tab so no muscle memory forms, a right swipe at the first segment collides with the iOS back gesture, the hand-off needs gesture-handler or Reanimated and would breach the zero-import crash containment, it implies a sliding tab transition against the instant-switch rule, and VoiceOver users gain nothing. The tab bar stays the one way to change pages. See [SegmentPager](SegmentPager.md).
