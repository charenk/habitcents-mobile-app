# DockCard (components/today/DockCard.tsx)

## Direction (current)
The one STRUCTURE both of Today's docks take: a white rounded-rect shell at `radii.feature` holding a field on the left at `radii.control` and a fixed 44pt, and a round plus on the right that stretches to the same height, 66pt outer in every case, so the dock's top edge lands at one y as the pager swipes. Two skins on that structure: the solid tone (1px cloud edge, snow field, filled sage plus) is the composer, [QuickLogRow](QuickLogRow.md); the dashed tone (1.5pt dashed cloudDashed edge, no fill behind either the field or the plus, sage glyph) is "add another", [BreakHabitRow](BreakHabitRow.md).

## States
Field pressed is cloud in both tones; at rest it is snow in the solid tone and unfilled in the dashed one. Filled plus resting (sage) and pressed (pressed sage); plain plus unfilled at rest, cloud pressed. The plus is hidden from assistive tech on both panes (UX-055); the field carries the spoken name.

## Decisions
- 2026-09-10 (Charen): the shell stops being a pill. Shell `radii.feature` (20), field `radii.control` (10). Why: a fully round composer promised a shape nothing behind it kept, because the sheet it opens carries ordinary radii on its inputs and controls, so the corner changed the moment you tapped. The two values are concentric by construction, not chosen twice: 20 minus the shell's own 10pt padding is exactly 10. The plus stays a circle, which is a circle rather than a rounded rect and creates none of that mismatch. **Reverses the "pill corners" half of the 2026-09-07 decision below.** Cost, accepted knowingly: 20 is the switchers' radius (SpentKeptChips' chip is `radii.feature`, its track 20 plus padding), so the dock joins a family PATTERN_VOCABULARY had reserved for the two switchers. Taken because the dock is a bordered composer at the foot of the pane and the chips a segmented track at its head, they never adjoin, and concentric corners on existing tokens beat inventing a third radius to keep the families apart. Rejected: shell 14 with a field at 4, which stays clear of the switchers but reads as a card and leaves the field nearly square.
- 2026-09-10 (Charen): the field drops from 52 to 44, so the shell drops from 74 to 66, both docks together. Why: Charen asked for both to be more compact, and the 52 existed only for Kept's label-plus-caption at 51.5pt at the 1.5 cap. With the caption gone (see [BreakHabitRow](BreakHabitRow.md)) that constraint went with it, and the floor is now set twice over at the same number: Spent's 28pt serif amount at AmountDisplay's 1.3 cap is about 44pt of line box, and 44 is the touch-target minimum. The field is therefore allowed to be exactly 44 and never less, which `__tests__/dockGeometry.test.tsx` now pins as a floor rather than an equality.
- 2026-09-10 (Charen): the dashed tone carries no resting fill, on the field or the plus. Why: a grey slab inside a grey dashed outline was two containers saying one thing, and the dock is meant to be the quiet "add another" voice. The pressed swap to cloud survives on both halves: it is the house convention from Button.tsx, and it is the only answer this dock gives a finger before a sheet or the paywall arrives. Rejected: no press feedback at all, which would have made it the one primary affordance in the app that does not answer a touch.
- 2026-09-07 (Charen, later the same day): the shell and the plus each take a `tone`, and Kept's dock is dashed with a plain plus. Why: with both docks solid and sage, "Break your first habit" read as another add-expense composer; Charen wanted it structurally the same and visibly different. Dashed is the grammar the app already uses for "add another" (UpcomingList's add row, the watch nudge), and it had been on this affordance until that morning. The plain plus is the field's own snow with the label's own sage, the quietest treatment that still reads as a button; rejected: a white circle with a cloud border (reads as header chrome) and a dashed-outline circle (two nested dashed edges read busy). The dashed tone's 1.5pt edge is paid for out of its padding (9.5 instead of 10) so both tones stay 74pt outer; pinned by `__tests__/dockGeometry.test.tsx`. Reverses the "one solid shell" half of the decision below, not the "one structure" half.
- 2026-09-07 (Charen): one shell for both docks, pill corners. Why: the quick log and the break affordance were two objects at two heights one swipe apart, and ActionDock's own record admitted it. A composer grammar that is the same on every pane is what stops the chrome jumping. Pill rather than the switchers' rounded rect: PATTERN_VOCABULARY reserves the rounded-rect nesting rule for the two switchers, and Charen asked for fully round on the dock specifically.
- 2026-09-07: the field height is fixed, not a minimum. Why: a minimum only equalises the docks while their contents happen to be the same size, and they were not. 52 holds the 28pt serif amount at its 1.3 cap (about 44pt) and the Kept label plus caption at the 1.5 chrome cap (51.5pt), clears the 44pt target floor, and keeps the shell under the 77pt chips row. Pinned by `__tests__/dockGeometry.test.tsx`, which reads the constant rather than repeating it.
- 2026-09-07: the plus squares its width off the stretched height (`aspectRatio: 1`) rather than pinning a width. Carried over from QuickLogRow, where it kept the square square as the field grew under Dynamic Type; now it keeps the circle round for the same reason.

## Open
- Closed 2026-09-10: **the 44 holds at the cap.** The serif line box at AmountDisplay's 1.3 cap is an estimate from font metrics and the field is now exactly that number, so it was checked rather than assumed: iPhone 16 Plus simulator at `content_size accessibility-extra-extra-extra-large`, relaunched, both panes. Spent's amount and Kept's label each still sit on one line inside the field and neither shell grew. 48 stays the agreed fallback if a real device ever disagrees.
- Android clips text that exceeds a fixed height where iOS lets it spill. The tightest case is now Spent's amount rather than Kept's caption; a real Android pass is still owed.
