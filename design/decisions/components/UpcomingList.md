# UpcomingList (components/money/UpcomingList.tsx)

## Direction (current)
What is coming, and what it costs. One card in three rows at the top of the pane: the window it is describing on the left with the filter that decides it in the corner, the total, then the payments count with the add affordance beside it. Below it, one row per bill, soonest first, each pressable to edit. Every number is projected by `utils/recurring.ts`; nothing on this surface is invented or rounded into a claim. Amounts render unsigned, because nothing here has been spent yet and a minus sign would read as history.

The card never changes shape. Every row renders in every state, so the window-empty case is an honest `$0.00` over `0 payments` rather than a hole where two lines used to be.

## States
Vocabulary (ADR 0034). This component owns two of Upcoming's three; the pane's true **Zero** is handled by an early return to `EmptyState`.

- Zero: no recurring expense exists at all, so the card is dropped entirely for the 96pt calendar and "Know what's coming before it lands". Reach: Persona new user.
- Window-empty: something recurs, just not inside the picked window. Card at `$0.00` / `0 payments`, one body line beneath it, no CTA. Reach: Persona returning user, then tap 2w. Harder to reach by accident now: the pane opens on the narrowest window that has data (see [money](../modules/money.md)), so this state is mostly something a user chooses their way into.
- Live: the card at its total, then the rows. Reach: Persona returning user.
- Per row: default / pressed. A row whose bill lands more than once in the window also carries its multiplier.

## Decisions
- 2026-09-11 (Charen): **a row's big number is what the bill costs inside the window, not what one payment costs.** Why: the card counted occurrences and the rows counted bills, so at 90 days a headline of $6,643.14 sat above four rows summing to $2,214.38, with nothing on screen reconciling them. The visible symptom was that switching 1 month to 3 months changed the headline and left the list byte-identical, so the filter looked broken. Now the column of row numbers sums to exactly the headline, and every row moves when the window moves. A caption names the unit price where a bill lands more than once; where it lands once, which is every row at the narrow windows, the row is unchanged. Both numbers come from one helper pair in `utils/recurring.ts` (`upcomingItemPayments` / `upcomingItemWindowTotal`) that the card's own totals reduce over, so the two can no longer be computed from separate definitions that happen to agree. Rejected: keeping the unit price primary with the subtotal as caption, which reads closer to the edit sheet but leaves the column not adding up, and that arithmetic was the whole finding. Charen picked from both drawn.
- 2026-09-11: **the caption says "\u00D73 \u00B7 $2,100.00", not "\u00D73 \u00B7 $2,100.00 each".** Why: "each" read better and cost the row about 30pt, which truncated the schedule line beside it into "Monthly \u00B7 next Se...". Seen on device, not in a test. The multiplication sign already says this is the unit price, and a truncated date is a worse loss than an implied word. VoiceOver still hears it in full ("3 payments of $2,100.00"), where nothing is competing for width. The amount column also gained a cap so a long currency shrinks its own text rather than stealing the row.
- 2026-09-11: **the amber multi-payment pill is retired from this surface.** Why: it fired on 3 or more occurrences in a calendar MONTH while the multiplier fires on 2 or more in the WINDOW, so the pill could never appear without the multiplier also appearing, and when both did they stated two different counts in two different scopes. A weekly bill at the 1 month window would have shown "\u00D74" on the right and "5 payments in Sep" in the pill: both correct, neither reconcilable. `multiPaymentMonth` stays exported and fully tested in `utils/recurring.ts`; only this rendering retires.
- 2026-09-11: **each row states its timing once.** The right column's "in 21 days" went; the schedule line's absolute date stays, which is the form you check against payday, and the list is sorted soonest-first so relative order is already visible. `daysUntilLabel` keeps its home in `utils/recurring.ts` with a retirement comment, because `UpcomingItem.daysUntil` is still computed and used by `advancePastToday`. Its "Today" branch had been unreachable here since ADR 0024 anyway.
- 2026-09-11 (Charen): **the card is three rows, and the window filter moved to the top-right corner.** Why: the picker used to span the full width of the card above everything, where it read as chrome bolted on top rather than as a dial on the number. Beside a sentence-case "Next 30 days" label it reads as what it is. The filter uses `SegmentedControl`'s new compact quiet tone (ADR 0040), shows `2w / 1m / 3m` and speaks "2 weeks", via the new `labelSpoken`. See [SegmentedControl](SegmentedControl.md).
- 2026-09-11: **every row of the card renders in every state, so window-empty reads `$0.00` / `0 payments`.** Why: this is a behaviour decision wearing a layout's clothes. The filter is in row 1, so a card that collapsed its total when a window came up empty would shrink **under the finger that tapped it** and shove the list up, which is a worse defect than a truthful zero. It also makes the filter legible as a live control: tap `1m` and watch `$0.00` become the real number. It falls out with no new branch and no new string, because `upcomingWindowTotal([])` is 0 and `upcomingPaymentsCount(0, 0)` already drops its "from N bills" clause when the counts agree. Rejected: hiding the total and letting the card shrink to one line, which is the old dead-white problem in a new shape. Verified on device: the card's top and bottom edges are identical across a `1m` to `2w` tap.
- 2026-09-11: **the window label is a label, not an eyebrow.** Sentence case at `typeScale.caption`, no uppercase and no 0.88 tracking, which exists to open up capitals and reads loose without them. The string never changed: casing has lived in the stylesheet since UX-060 so a screen reader hears words. Named as a deviation in the PR body, since the pane's other eyebrows stay uppercase.
- 2026-09-11: **one add affordance, not two.** The window-empty body dropped its CTA; the dashed plus sits 40pt above it and does the same thing in the same words. The true-zero state keeps its CTA, which is the only action in an otherwise empty pane.
- 2026-09-11: **the "Scheduled" eyebrow is retired.** A section heading over the only list on the pane, directly under a card that already says what the pane is. The key stays in the catalog with a dated comment; only usage retires.
- 2026-09-11: **the object is a "bill".** The pane was using four nouns for one thing (bills, repeating expenses, upcoming expense, Scheduled). "Bill" is the shortest, the most concrete, was already in the count line, and is the word a user says out loud. `upcomingWindowEmptyBody` is the one value change; the sheet's three "upcoming expense" labels move together in the drawer round.

## Decisions (continued)
- 2026-09-11 (Charen): **the row's left column is three lines: the name, an elbow arrow with the date, then the cadence as a badge.** Why: Charen's annotation asked to replace the word "next" with a curved arrow and to move the cadence out of the sentence. The row now DRAWS `scheduleParts` and SPEAKS `describeSchedule`, which is the same visual-only decomposition `labelSpoken` and `badgeSpoken` established in ADR 0040: VoiceOver still hears "Weekly, Saturdays, next Sep 18" while the row shows an arrow, a date and a pill. The glyph is `CornerDownRight`, an elbow that descends and turns right, so it draws the relation *this bill leads to this date*. Rejected: `ChevronRight`, already the trailing edit affordance on this same row, and `Repeat`, which already means "recurring" on `ExpenseRow` one segment away. A straight arrow shares the chevron's axis and reads as navigation.
- 2026-09-11: **weekly and biweekly rules name their weekday on the date, not in the badge.** Their qualifier ("Saturdays") lives in the sentence, and a badge reading "Weekly \u00B7 Saturdays" is a sentence in a pill. So the date becomes "Sat, Sep 18" for those two rule types only, and the badge stays one word. Nothing is lost from speech, where the full sentence still runs.
- 2026-09-11: **the cadence badge is local styles, not a new `ui/Badge`.** A primitive would ship with one consumer: `TierBadge`'s meaning is shape plus label, and `SegmentedControl`'s badge carries selection-dependent tone. It borrows SegmentedControl's geometry (minHeight 18, radius 999, 11pt uiBold), `slate` on `cloud` because `mistText` measures 4.06:1 on a cloud fill, and a neutral fill because cadence is a fact rather than a judgment. See Open for the extraction candidate.
- 2026-09-11: **the row aligns to the top unconditionally**, and the accessibility-only `rowStacked` branch collapsed into the base value. With three lines on the left, the amount belongs beside the NAME rather than floating at the vertical centre of the block, which is what that branch's own comment already argued for.
- 2026-09-11: **the rows stack at accessibility text sizes**, the same reflow `ExpenseRow` takes and for the same reason: the bill's name and its windowed subtotal are both content and both want the row. The schedule line is metadata and caps. See [ExpenseRow](ExpenseRow.md).
- 2026-09-11: **the card's two chrome lines cap at 1.5x text scale; the amount does not.** Seen at XXXL on device: the count line carried `numberOfLines={1}` and cropped to a band of half-glyphs, and the window label wrapped to three lines beside a one-line filter. The count line wraps now and the row grows, which is what the 44pt button beside it can afford. 1.5 is the app's existing ceiling for chrome text (`SegmentedControl`, the tab bar). The total itself stays uncapped: it is the content, not the chrome, and a user at AX sizes wants it big.

## Open
- One geometry, three implementations: this badge, `SegmentedControl`'s internal one and `TierBadge` all describe the same pill. A `ui/Badge` is the right extraction the moment a second label-only host appears; today it would have one.
- At the 90-day window the total renders a quarter's bills in the same 36pt serif slot that means "this month" everywhere else in the app. The label above it makes it honest; the typography still makes it feel monthly.
- Checked at XXXL on device 2026-09-11: the corner filter holds, one line, no wrap, no collision, which was the risk this design carried. The card gets very tall because the serif total wraps, but nothing clips or overlaps. The rows' own accessibility-size behaviour was fixed in the same-day sweep that followed (see [ExpenseRow](ExpenseRow.md)).
