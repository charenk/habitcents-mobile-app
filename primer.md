## Previous state (2026-09-11)

The Upcoming wave: Money > Upcoming was audited, redesigned and rebuilt across eight stacked PRs (#165 through #172), all merged to main the same day. 120 suites, 1325 tests, tsc clean, lint 0 errors, every PR OTA-ELIGIBLE. TestFlight build 25 started from main (`4da6f4d`) with auto-submit.

- **The pane's arithmetic closes at three levels.** The card counted occurrences while the rows counted bills, so a headline of $6,643.14 sat above rows summing to $2,214.38 with nothing on screen reconciling them. Now a row's number is its windowed subtotal, both numbers come from one helper pair in `utils/recurring.ts` (`upcomingItemPayments` / `upcomingItemWindowTotal`) that the card's totals reduce over, and `utils/upcomingGroups.ts` groups by calendar month with per-month scoping: rows sum to their header, headers sum to the card, the card is still `upcomingWindowTotal` untouched.
- **A bill may say it knows the month but not the day** (ADR 0042, which amends 0024 and 0041). `Expense.datePrecision` is `'day'` or `'month'`, absent meaning `'day'`. Under `'month'` the stored date is the LAST day of the named month and the rule is `{ type: 'monthly', monthDay: 'last' }`, so it buckets into exactly that month and stays live for all of it. Four guards consult precision: `admits` (month overlap at the window's far edge), `advancePastToday` (must not advance), `occurrencesToMaterialize` (returns `[]`), and `scheduleParts` (qualifier forced to `null`, so VoiceOver never hears "Last day" for a day nobody asserted). `datePrecision` is a read-path contract now: every surface that renders or compares a date must consult it, and the next such surface will not know to ask.
- **An unknown-day bill reaches Spent by asking.** The materializer refuses to write a spend on a day nobody asserted, which left the ledger never recording the payment. The current month's row carries a check; it writes a child dated today in the materializer's own shape (`source: 'recurring'`, the parent's `parentId`), and `dropPaidMonths` takes that month out of Upcoming keyed on `(parentId, month)`. Current month only: a future month has not happened, and offering it there would mean asking for a day inside a month that has not started.
- **The sheet grew two controls and a picker.** Yearly draws a month rail and day grid (`components/money/MonthDayPicker.tsx`, built entirely from `Chip` so `package.json` stays untouched and the change stays OTA-eligible); a bill can carry its own glyph (`expenseGlyph`, `emoji?` on Expense, 24-cell grid with a reset cell); monthly and annual offer "I don't know the day", one-time offers "Pick a date".
- **Rows survive accessibility text sizes.** At AX1 and above `ExpenseRow`, `HabitLeakRow`, `WhereItWentCard` and Upcoming's rows stack (name, secondary line, amount) rather than competing for one row; `utils/textScale.ts` owns the threshold and the 1.5 chrome cap. `EmojiTile`'s glyph is the app's only `allowFontScaling={false}`, because nobody reads it.
- **Owed on the next TestFlight build (device pass):** the blank date line on an unknown-day row (recorded as Open: with the arrow and date both gone there is no visible difference between "the user did not know" and "the date failed to render"; the alternative is drawing the month name), AX5 (only eyeballed at AX3), Android Dynamic Type, and everything still owed from build 22.

The reminders paragraph below is HISTORY now (shipped 2026-09-26); its two traps were both real and both closed.

## Dev

Quick start: `npm install` then `npm run web` / `npm run ios`. Tests: `npm test`. Type-check: `npx tsc --noEmit`.
To see the app populated with demo data: `git checkout dev/seed-data` then run (SEED_DEV_DATA flag in `utils/devSeed.ts`); return to `main` for a clean first-run.
